# Case Study 5: Nuclear Science – ISM Adaptation

**Sector:** Nuclear / Science & Technology

**Scenario:** ANSTO needed to adapt the ISM to meet nuclear security objectives (aligned with IAEA guidance).

**Key ISM Principles Applied:**
- PRO-01 (Secure lifecycle)
- IDE-03 (CIA requirements)
- PRO-06 (Vulnerability management)
- PRO-08 (Data encryption)

**Outcome:** ISM adapted for nuclear security context. Both prescriptive and performance‑based measures implemented. Aligned with international nuclear security standards.
