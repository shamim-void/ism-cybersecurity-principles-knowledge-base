# Case Study 1: Government Security Accreditation

**Sector:** Federal Government

**Scenario:** A government department needed security accreditation for multiple systems.

**Key ISM Principles Applied:**
- GOV-01, GOV-03, GOV-05 (Governance)
- IDE-01, IDE-02 (Asset & criticality identification)
- PRO-01 (Secure lifecycle)

**Outcome:** Standardised security documentation across all systems, clear risk acceptance processes, and demonstrable ISM compliance.
