# Case Study 2: Critical Infrastructure Security Overhaul

**Sector:** Critical Infrastructure (3,500+ staff, 29 locations)

**Scenario:** A national infrastructure provider required a complete security architecture review.

**Key ISM Principles Applied:**
- GOV-02 (CISO leadership)
- PRO-02, PRO-04 (Secure by design, attack surface reduction)
- PRO-06 (Vulnerability management)
- DET-01 (Centralised logging)
- RES-01 (Incident planning)

**Outcome:** Comprehensive maturity assessment, prioritised remediation roadmap, and enhanced national resilience.
