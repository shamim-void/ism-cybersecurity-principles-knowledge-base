# Case Study 4: State Government – Essential Eight Compliance

**Sector:** State Government

**Scenario:** Agency required Essential Eight Maturity Level 1 compliance under NSW Cyber Security Policy.

**Key ISM Principles Applied:**
- GOV-01, GOV-03 (Accountability, risk management)
- PRO-14 (Awareness training)
- RES-01 (Incident planning)
- REC-01 (Business resumption)

**Outcome:** Annual attestation capability, integration with enterprise risk frameworks, and demonstrated Essential Eight maturity.
